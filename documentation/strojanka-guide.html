Install, update, revert

Everything goes through one program: StrojankaInstaller.exe.

Updating

  1. Download StrojankaInstaller.exe from the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Run it and choose Update. Your token, database and settings are kept.
  3. A running Strojanka Server is closed for you first. Tick Start Strojanka now at the end to start it again.

Going back: Revert update

For 3 days after an update, the setup shows a Revert update card. It puts back the version the update replaced. Your settings, watch lists and trades stay. Open the setup from the downloaded installer, or from Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Strojanka > … > Modify.

If the update also upgraded the database, the copy saved just before the update is put back. Anything recorded after the update is kept in a file called strojanka.db.after-update-<date> next to the database.

Start with Windows and shortcuts

  • Start with Windows (on by default): Strojanka Server opens minimized every time you sign in, and waits up to three minutes for the internet before connecting to Discord. You can switch it off in Task Manager > Startup apps, or in the setup.
  • Start menu and Desktop shortcuts are options on the Welcome page. An update keeps the choices you made before.
  • It starts when you sign in, not before. If Windows restarts on its own at night, the bot runs again once someone signs in.

Adding stocks

Strojanka uses Yahoo Finance tickers. A US stock has no ending; other exchanges need one.

/monitor stock:NVDA        US stock, no ending
/monitor stock:OTP.BD      OTP Bank on the Budapest exchange
/monitor stock:DHL.DE      DHL Group on Xetra
/monitor stock:HEAL.L      a London listing

If you get the ending wrong

The bot doesn't just say "not found". It suggests the real listings:

  • OTP → OTP.BD, DHL → DHL.DE: a ticker without an ending gets matches from the main exchanges.
  • HEAL.G → HEAL.L: an ending that doesn't exist gets the list of real endings plus suggestions.

Type /market to see every exchange's ending.

ETFs

ETFs work exactly like stocks: SPY, VOO, CSPX.L, SXR8.DE. Pick the listing you actually bought, because the same fund trades in different currencies on different exchanges.

  • Prices are in the listing's currency. London prices quoted in pence are converted to pounds, so type amounts in pounds too.
  • News isn't used for ETFs, since fund headlines are mostly about the fund company.
  • Amounts can be typed the European way: 1.234,50€ is 1234.50.

Company names appear in the watched-stock tables and in the alerts, for example NVDA (NVIDIA).

Markets and price checks

A stock's price is only checked while its market is open.

The rule

Market open→ checked every poll interval (15 min by default)
Market closes→ one more check about 10 minutes later for the closing prices
Market closed→ not checked until it opens again

When every market is closed

At the weekend, or at night when all your watched stocks' markets are closed, nothing is fetched at all. The server window then shows this on the Last price check card instead of a time that keeps getting older:

Markets closed
Tokyo opens Mon 02:00

The admin panel shows the same, and the log says it once.

  • Checking starts again about 30 seconds after the first market opens, without waiting for the next regular check.
  • Adding a stock (/monitor, or a /buy of a stock you weren't watching) or removing one recalculates this straight away. If you add a Tokyo stock on Saturday while you only watch Budapest stocks, the next check moves to Tokyo's opening, and the log says next price check moved earlier: Tokyo opens Mon 02:00.
  • The console's check command always checks everything, open or closed.
Public holidays aren't known Hours are Monday to Friday with no holiday list, so on a holiday a market shows as open and is checked as usual. That costs a few requests but does no harm.

PEAK and SUPPORT alerts

Alerts arrive as direct messages from the bot.

AlertMeansWho gets it
PEAKThe stock has probably reached the top of its current swing: time to sell.Only watchers who own shares. The message says how many you have.
SUPPORTThe stock has probably fallen to a floor: time to buy.Everyone watching the stock, including people who already own shares. The message doesn't mention your shares.
FCX (Freeport-McMoRan) has reached PEAK. You have 0.858291457 shares. It is time to sell.
FCX (Freeport-McMoRan) has reached SUPPORT level. It is time to buy.

Limits on how often

  • At most one alert per price swing.
  • At most one PEAK and one SUPPORT per stock every 3 days.
  • No alerts from price data older than 5 days.
  • A SUPPORT alert is held back when there is red-flag news about the company. The log says so.

Each alert also carries a line on the news of the last three days (not for ETFs) and Pythia's view, once the AI model is trained.

Read the honest test /report compares the bot's past calls with a call on a random day. When its number isn't clearly higher, the report says no proven edge yet: treat that stock's alerts as hints only.

Price and safety alerts

Two alerts you control yourself, new in 0.2.1.

Price alerts: /watch

/watch stock:NVDA price:250      one message when NVDA gets to $250
/watch                           your price alerts
/unwatch stock:NVDA              remove all of NVDA's
/unwatch stock:NVDA price:250    remove just that one
  • If the price is below 250 now, it waits for a rise to 250 or more; if above, for a fall to 250 or less.
  • It fires once, then it's done.
  • The stock doesn't need to be on your watch list. It's checked anyway, but you then get only the price alert, no PEAK/SUPPORT.
  • Checked every poll interval (15 minutes by default) while the market is open, so a quick spike between two checks can be missed. At most 25 per person.
🔔 NVDA (NVIDIA) reached your price: $251.30 (you asked for $250.00 or higher).

Safety alerts: /safety

For stocks you hold. When one that has been comfortably in profit falls back close to your buying price, you get a warning while selling would still leave a little profit after the sale fee.

In profit→ falls →Warn at→ still falling →Break-even= what you paid + the fee
  • Break-even = your average cost plus the fee for one sale (0.20 by default, in the stock's currency), per share. Selling below it loses money.
  • Warn at = about two normal days' moves of that stock above break-even (1.5% to 8%). A calm stock is warned close to the line, a jumpy one earlier.
  • Only while the price is falling. A stock near break-even that is climbing again stays quiet.
  • Once per fall. The next warning comes only after the price has climbed back up well above the line. Right after a purchase there's nothing to protect yet.
  • If it drops straight past the line between two checks, the message says it has dropped below your buying price and what selling now would lose.
🛡️ NVDA (NVIDIA) is falling and is close to your buying price: $120.45 now, break-even $120.07. If you don't want to risk a loss, now is the time to sell: your 3 shares would still make about +$1.15 after the fee. You don't have to, though: a fall can turn around, and it's your call.
CommandAs textWhat it does
/safetysafetyEvery holding's price, break-even, warning price and state.
/safety fee:0.50safety fee 0.50What one sale costs you.
/safety alerts:offsafety offSilences all safety alerts (on turns them back on).
/safety stock:NVDA alerts:offsafety off NVDASilences them for one stock only.

Portfolio and history

Record your trades and the bot keeps the books.

/buy  stock:FCX shares:0.858291457 paid:59.78$       paid = the total, not per share
/sell stock:FCX shares:0.858291457 received:67.87$
CommandShows
/portfolioWhat you hold now: shares, average cost, price, value, weight and profit or loss, with totals per currency.
/history, /history stock:FCXEvery buy and sell, newest first, with the profit of each sale. For one stock, also the shares held after each trade.
/statusYour watch list with prices, trends, holdings and your balance.
/profitRealized profit (from sales) and unrealized profit (shares you still hold).

With several buys at different prices, profit uses your average cost. Amounts in different currencies are never added together; each currency gets its own total.

Backups

Everything the bot knows is in one database file. A backup is a checked copy of it.

Making a backup

  • Press Backup in the server window's top bar, or type backup in its console, or send admin backup in Discord.
  • The file is called strojanka-<date>.strojanka_backup and goes into the backups folder of the install folder.
  • To keep backups on another drive or in a cloud folder, set backup_folder in the settings file. That's the safer place for them.
  • The Database card shows how long ago the last backup was, or never backed up ⚠.

Reminders

Last backup→ 3 weeks pass →First reminder→ then every 2 days until you make a backup

Admins get the reminder as a Discord message and, if an address is set, by email. See email reminders.

Restoring

  1. Run the setup and choose Change settings, or start a new install.
  2. Under Database, pick Restore a backup and choose the .strojanka_backup file.
  3. Finish the setup. A database already in that place is not deleted; it's kept beside it as .before-restore-<date>.

Uninstalling never deletes the backups folder.

Email reminders and SMTP

You don't need to create a mail server. Strojanka uses the one your email provider already runs.

An SMTP server is the server that sends email. Gmail, Outlook and the others each run one. Strojanka logs in to yours with your address and a password, and sends the reminder from your account to itself.

Setting it up with Gmail (free)

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account, if it isn't on yet. Google only offers app passwords with it on.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type a name such as Strojanka and click Create.
  3. Google shows a 16-character password. Put it in the setup as the app password, next to your Gmail address. Your normal Gmail password won't work here.
  4. Send admin email test to the bot and check that the message arrives.
CommandWhat it does
admin email you@example.comSets or changes the address for reminders.
admin email testSends a test email now.
admin email offStops email reminders (Discord reminders stay).

The server is filled in from your address for Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo and iCloud. For another provider, find "SMTP settings" in its help pages and set smtp_server in the settings file, for example smtps://smtp.example.com:465. The mailbox password lives in smtp_password.

Why not run your own mail server? It's possible (hMailServer, for example), but most home internet providers block outgoing mail, and Gmail and others usually send mail from a home connection to spam or reject it. Your provider's server avoids all that and costs nothing.

Politician trades

Members of the US Congress must disclose their stock trades. Strojanka reads the official filings once a day.

  • House of Representatives: read from the House Clerk's official site, including its PDF trade reports. Scanned paper filings (about 15%) are images and are skipped.
  • Senate: its site refuses connections from outside the US. From Hungary it answers "refused (HTTP 403)" every time. Strojanka still tries daily and logs it, but expect Senate trades only if the bot runs on a US connection.
  • Disclosures can come up to ~45 days after the trade, so "new" means newly disclosed.
  • The very first check stores what it finds without posting it, so the channel isn't flooded. Every filing after that is posted.
/politicians                    recent trades, everyone's
/politicians name:Pelosi        one politician
/monitor politician:Pelosi      a direct message when they disclose a trade
/remove politician:Pelosi
admin channel <channel ID>      where new filings (and public arena bots) are posted

A last name is enough. Every word you type must appear in the name.

The arena

Thirteen trading bots, each with $1,000 of play money and one fixed strategy.

Training→ passes the history test →Proving→ 40 good live days →Public

What happens when a bot fails

SituationWhat happens
Fails the history testStays in training. Every 7 days all bots are tested again with the newest prices; if it passes, it starts proving with a fresh $1,000.
Proving, but not good enoughStays in proving, judged again every day on its latest 40 trading days. It doesn't start over, so it can still make it.
Public, but falls >25% or lags Atlas by 10 points over 60 daysBack to proving, announced in the channel.
Public, but fails the weekly history testBack to training, announced in the channel.
"Training" doesn't mean learning Each bot's rules are fixed, for example "Ares holds a stock while its 20-day average is above its 100-day average". Nothing adjusts those settings. The weekly retest only adds one week to about 8 years of history, so a bot that failed will almost always fail again. Only Pythia and Sibylla really retrain: the AI model behind them is trained again every week, so their results can change.

Follow it with /arena and /arena bot:Diana in Discord, or in the Arena tab of the server window (Ctrl+2), which also shows the private bots. Play money only, not investment advice.

If Strojanka closes by itself

The log now records why it stopped. Open strojanka.log with the Log button.

In the logMeaning
Windows is shutting down or restarting, or an installer or Windows Update asked running programs to closeNot a crash: Windows ended it. Windows Update restarts at night unless you set active hours. With Start with Windows on, it comes back when you sign in.
the computer is going to sleepNothing runs while the computer sleeps. Change Settings > System > Power if the bot should keep running.
CRASH: lines, or the previous run … did not shut down cleanlyA real crash. Open a GitHub issue and attach strojanka.log, plus strojanka-crash.log and strojanka-crash.dmp from the install folder if they exist.
