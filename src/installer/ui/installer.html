Install Strojanka

Strojanka watches your stocks and sends you a Discord message when one reaches a peak or a support level.

UpdateInstall the new version and keep your token, database and settings.

UninstallRemove Strojanka from this computer.
Start with Windows opens Strojanka minimized when you sign in, so your alerts keep running after a restart, for example after a Windows update. You can turn it off later here or in Task Manager > Startup apps.
